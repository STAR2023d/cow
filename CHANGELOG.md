# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Monthly Report: export the month as a PDF (totals with last month, cost per litre, spending by category, earnings per buyer)
- Financial Statement: export the period shown as a PDF (saved to Documents/Mwauras Farm and opened in the PDF viewer)
- Milk Entry: edit the litres and buyer of an entry (tap the row or the pencil icon)
- Milk Entry: delete a wrong entry from the history list (bin icon, asks first)
- AI: opt-in live check (`test/live/ai_live_test.dart`) sends a short request and one vet case to each provider that has settings; skipped in a plain `flutter test`
- AI: Gemini time limit raised from 45 s to 90 s (the vet case timed out at 45 s in the live check)
- AI: Gemini, Claude (Anthropic) and OpenAI / OpenAI-compatible providers over plain HTTP; `google_generative_ai` removed
- AI: choose the provider and model with `AI_PROVIDER` and per-provider settings; incomplete settings report "not set up" without a request
- AI: cross-provider fallback (`AI_FALLBACK_PROVIDERS`) when a provider stays busy or unreachable; blocked answers are never retried elsewhere
- Monthly Report: "Earnings per buyer" panel (amount, litres and share of milk income per buyer; entries without a buyer grouped as "Not recorded")
- Schema v10: new `Calves` table and optional `buyer` column on milk entries (migration from v9 tested)
- Calf Register: add calves bought in or born before the app, and record birth weight, weaning date and notes for any calf (see `docs/CALVES.md`)
- Milk Entry: optional Buyer field, shown in the history list; leaving it blank never clears a buyer saved earlier
- Roadmap: "Earnings per buyer" was ticked by mistake and is now unticked (the report is not built yet)
- Calf Register screen (drawer): every calf recorded through breeding cycles with dam, birth date, age, sex and outcome (kept, sold with amount, or awaiting)
- Dashboard "Health reminders" card for follow-ups overdue, due today or within 14 days
- Preset chips in the Log Health Record dialog (Vaccination, Tick control, Deworming, Hoof care, Vet check) with suggested follow-up dates; added "Tick control" and "Hoof care" record types
- Dashboard shows a "Milk on hold" card listing cows under milk withdrawal
- Milk Entry screen shows an orange "Milk on hold" banner while the cow is under milk withdrawal
- Rewrote README and added a roadmap
- Removed unused `models/milk.dart` and `models/milk_entry_enum.dart`
- Fixed session totals in analytics (case mismatch); added `MilkSession.afternoon`
- Analytics screen now shows only today's milk entries (was all-time)

- Added `docs/AI.md`

- Fixed `monthly_report_test.dart` to use dates in the past (the default price is dated "now")
