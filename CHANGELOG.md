# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Kiswahili UI, stage 1: language switch in the drawer (English / Kiswahili, remembered), Flutter localizations with ARB files (`lib/l10n/`), and the dashboard, drawer, Log milk sheet, milk price screens and undo messages translated. Kiswahili needs review by a native speaker (see `docs/SWAHILI.md`)
- AI: default Gemini model is now `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` (`gemini-3.8-flash` gave no answer within 90 s in the live check; flash-lite answered in seconds)
- Milk price setting: Milk price screen (drawer → Money and dashboard card) to change the price per litre with an "applies from" day and a history (delete with Undo). Prices apply from the start of their day and a second price on the same day replaces the first. Until a price is set, the dashboard asks for it (earnings were silently using KSh 50). The milk sheet's Save button shows the value, e.g. "≈ KSh 852"
- Undo for deletes: milk entries, feeding records, health records and calf details delete at once and show "... deleted" with Undo for 6 seconds (the milk entry confirm box is gone). Deleting a cow still asks first
- Light theme (now the default, easier to read outdoors) and a Light / Dark / Phone switch at the bottom of the drawer, remembered between runs (`shared_preferences`). Colours now come from the theme: about 330 fixed colours in 29 files replaced by theme text colours and named colours (`context.farm.good/warning/danger/info/special/muted/faint`, `lib/theme/app_theme.dart`); light-mode colours pass a 4.5:1 contrast test on white. Quick-action circles are 48 px and tinted
- One format everywhere (`lib/utils/format.dart`): money as `KSh 5,500` (statement lines with cents), litres as `6.5 L`, dates as `14 Nov 2026` (`Sat 14 Nov` for things due soon, `14 Nov 2026, 07:30` for milk entries). PDFs use the same dates and grouped amounts; PDF file names keep `2026-11-01`
- Log milk: a big button on the dashboard (and in the drawer) opens a whole-herd sheet. Pick today, yesterday or another day and the session once (suggested from the time of day), type each cow's litres and save once. Each cow shows her usual yield for that session, any figure already saved, and a milk-hold warning; figures far above usual (or over 40 L) ask "Save anyway?"
- Drawer: grouped into Herd, Daily care and Money; removed Home, Search, Dashboard and Settings (they did nothing); added Breeding and Financial Statement; the drawer now closes before a screen opens, so Back returns Home
- Monthly Report: export the month as a PDF (totals with last month, cost per litre, spending by category, earnings per buyer)
- Financial Statement: export the period shown as a PDF (saved to Documents/Mwauras Farm and opened in the PDF viewer)
- Milk Entry: edit the litres and buyer of an entry (tap the row or the pencil icon)
- Milk Entry: delete a wrong entry from the history list (bin icon)
- AI: opt-in live check (`test/live/ai_live_test.dart`) sends a short request and one vet case to each provider that has settings; skipped in a plain `flutter test`
- AI: Gemini time limit raised from 45 s to 90 s (the vet case timed out at 45 s in the live check)
- AI: Gemini, Claude (Anthropic) and OpenAI / OpenAI-compatible providers over plain HTTP; `google_generative_ai` removed
- AI: choose the provider and model with `AI_PROVIDER` and per-provider settings; incomplete settings report "not set up" without a request
- AI: cross-provider fallback (`AI_FALLBACK_PROVIDERS`) when a provider stays busy or unreachable; blocked answers are never retried elsewhere
- Monthly Report: "Earnings per buyer" panel (amount, litres and share of milk income per buyer; entries without a buyer grouped as "Not recorded")
- Schema v10: new `Calves` table and optional `buyer` column on milk entries (migration from v9 tested)
- Calf Register: add calves bought in or born before the app, and record birth weight, weaning date and notes for any calf (see `docs/CALVES.md`)
- Milk Entry: optional Buyer field, shown in the history list; leaving it blank never clears a buyer saved earlier
- Roadmap: "Earnings per buyer" was ticked by mistake and is now unticked (the report is not built yet)
- Calf Register screen (drawer): every calf recorded through breeding cycles with dam, birth date, age, sex and outcome (kept, sold with amount, or awaiting)
- Dashboard "Health reminders" card for follow-ups overdue, due today or within 14 days
- Preset chips in the Log Health Record dialog (Vaccination, Tick control, Deworming, Hoof care, Vet check) with suggested follow-up dates; added "Tick control" and "Hoof care" record types
